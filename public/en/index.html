Hi, I’m Mila Korshunova, a screenwriter and filmmaker working across short films, music videos, and experimental forms.

I began as a screenwriter, writing for short films and creating music videos in collaboration with other artists. One of my latest works is Cutouts, a handmade stop-motion short based on my own script, which has screened at several international film festivals. I’m currently developing new films in both animation and live action.
